# 🎬 CINE-Odia

**CINE-Odia** is a digital platform dedicated to **Odia cinema**, designed to provide a simple and organized way to discover and explore Odia movies. The platform presents essential movie information such as **Title, Release, Rate, Genre, Cast, and Watch links** for trailers or available movies.

The project aims to bring Odia movie information into one centralized platform while making it easy for users to search, explore, and learn about the rich history and content of the Odia film industry.
