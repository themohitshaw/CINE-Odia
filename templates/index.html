About CINE-Odia

Odia Cinema Deserves A Home Online

CINE-Odia was built for one reason: Odia cinema deserves a proper home online.

Odia movies rarely get the same visibility as mainstream Bollywood or Tollywood content. Finding a good Ollywood film to watch, or discovering something new based on what you already love, often means digging through scattered sources. CINE-Odia brings that discovery experience into one place.

Explore

A searchable collection of Odia films with details on cast, genre, release year, and ratings.

Get Suggestions

Tell us a few movies you already love, and we recommend similar ones based on genre, cast, and era.

Always Growing

Built to grow — more films and more data get added as the collection expands.

Why Odia Cinema

Ollywood has a rich, decades-long history of storytelling — from classic dramas to modern releases — that reflects Odisha's culture and language. CINE-Odia exists to celebrate that catalog and make it easier for fans, old and new, to explore it.

This project started as a way to combine a love for cinema with code — built independently, one feature at a time.
